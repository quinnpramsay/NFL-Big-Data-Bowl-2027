# Hypothesis #1

My first hypothesis is that we should not be trying to jsut simply do an A predicts B project but instead find underpirced/undervalued athletic ability within a positiong roup. I feel that the most effective position group that can be used for this is defensive backs, as you can have a large dataset than most position groups, and also can easily performing grouping analysis with outside coverage, slot/nickle coverage, and safety coverage, creating 4 true analysis. Also they seem to have the best independent ability within the game, they are not constraint on the quality of quarterback throwing to them. Change of direction is not going to be intuitive and will probably be something most models try to predict. But instead of asking, 'Does change-of-direction ability at the NFL Combine predict NFL performance?' I want to investigate whether less obvious combinations of athletic traits can identify undervalued prospects whose physical profiles suggest greater NFL potential than their draft position reflects.

This also leaves the possibility of different athletic performances could be derived differently, where straight line speed players are over drafted, and acceleration and agility players are underdrafted.
